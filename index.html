Our North Star

Vision & Mission

We are MER — a bold startup with a big dream. We are committed to enhancing Ethiopian dairy production and accelerating the nation's journey toward digital excellence and industrial leadership.

Under the visionary guidance of Prime Minister Dr. Abiy Ahmed, Ethiopia is rapidly advancing. MER is proud to be an active driver of this transformation — blending modern technology, sustainable practices, and deep local passion to unlock the full potential of Ethiopia's dairy industry.

Ethiopia's Footprint

Ethiopia in the Horn & Africa

A nation of ancient heritage, modern ambition, and growing influence across the continent and beyond.

🌍

The Cradle of Humanity

Ethiopia is home to some of the oldest hominid fossils, including the 3.2-million-year-old Lucy. This deep heritage gives Ethiopia a unique cultural weight in the Horn of Africa and across the continent.

🏛️

The Horn's Anchor

As the most populous country in the Horn of Africa, Ethiopia plays a pivotal role in regional stability, trade, and diplomacy. Its influence extends from the Red Sea to the Great Lakes, shaping the future of East Africa.

📈

Africa's Rising Star

With one of the fastest-growing economies in Africa, Ethiopia is becoming a hub for manufacturing, technology, and agriculture. Its commitment to digital transformation positions it as a leader in the continent's fourth industrial revolution.

🤝

Unity in Diversity

Home to over 80 ethnic groups and languages, Ethiopia is a testament to unity in diversity. This cultural richness fuels creativity, resilience, and a spirit of innovation that inspires all of Africa.

🌾

Agricultural Powerhouse

Ethiopia is the largest livestock producer in Africa, with over 70 million cattle. The dairy sector alone holds immense potential to feed the nation, create jobs, and become a major export powerhouse.

💡

The Digital Dawn

With initiatives like "Digital Ethiopia 2025," the country is leapfrogging into the digital age. This is creating unprecedented opportunities for startups like MER to revolutionize traditional industries.

Our Journey

Our Story & Ambition

A new chapter for Ethiopian dairy — rooted in heritage, powered by innovation.

Ethiopia is rising. With unstoppable momentum and boundless potential, our nation stands at the dawn of a new era. Rooted in rich agricultural heritage and fueled by rapid growth in services and industry, Ethiopia is transforming under the bold and visionary leadership of Prime Minister Dr. Abiy Ahmed. The commitment to digitalize the nation has ignited a powerful movement — one that dreams of positioning Ethiopia as Africa's leading force in agro-industry and technology.

At MER, we are a startup with a big dream. We do not wish to merely observe this transformation. We choose to be an active architect of it. We are deeply committed to enhancing dairy production across Ethiopia. Dairy is far more than milk — it is nutrition for our children, sustainable income for millions of smallholder farmers, and a cornerstone for building a modern, self-reliant agro-industrial economy.

By blending cutting-edge digital technologies, sustainable farming practices, and world-class processing standards, MER is creating a new future for Ethiopian dairy. We are empowering farmers with smart tools, building efficient value chains, reducing losses, and transforming raw potential into premium, globally competitive dairy products.

We believe in an Ethiopia where innovation meets tradition — where smallholder farmers thrive through technology, where young people find opportunity in modern agriculture, and where the nation proudly stands as a beacon of progress across Africa.

This is our purpose. This is our passion. We are not just enhancing dairy production. We are helping to write a new chapter in Ethiopia's story — one of prosperity, innovation, and national pride. We are determined to leave a lasting fingerprint on this great nation's journey toward greatness.

Together with the people and government of Ethiopia, MER will help turn ambitious dreams into reality. A digitally empowered, industrially strong, and agriculturally prosperous Ethiopia — not just for today, but for generations to come. This is more than business. This is our contribution to a greater Ethiopia.
